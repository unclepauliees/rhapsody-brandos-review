01 — The Manifesto

Every medium was invented somewhere. Broadcast was invented in a studio. Social was invented in a feed. The next one gets invented in orbit — and it hasn't been invented yet.

We are not selling access to space. We are building the first instrument for making media there — and deciding, for now, who gets to play it.

The old world composes. It plans a mission for seven years, freezes it at launch-day technology, and calls the finished thing a masterpiece before anyone has seen it. We work the other way. We reconfigure on orbit in weeks. We respond. We improvise — not as a metaphor, but as the literal behavior of the machine.

Which means every work made here happens once. The pass over the city, the light at that exact angle, the take at that exact moment — unrepeatable, un-fakeable, and impossible to generate after the fact.

In a world where anything can be generated infinitely, the live and the unrepeatable become the last real luxury.

We are not looking for customers. We are looking for the first generation — the houses, artists and studios who would rather found a medium than buy a billboard, and who understand that a place in an origin story outlasts any campaign.

Nobody has played this before.

02 — The Spine

Media has had two movements. We are writing the third.

Every medium is defined by the physics of where it is made. The next one is made somewhere with new physics — and nobody has written its grammar yet.

Movement I

Broadcast

Composed.

One voice, scripted years out, Earth-bound. The masterpiece is finished before it airs.

Movement II

Social

Algorithmic.

Many voices, still terrestrial, still ruled by the feed. Volume without permanence.

Movement III

Space-native

Improvised.

A new instrument in a place with new physics. Live, reconfigurable, unrepeatable, un-fakeable.

The ethos

Rhapsody is the first instrument for making media in orbit — where the method is improvisation, and no performance can be played twice. It exists for the culture-makers who would rather found a medium than buy a billboard. The soul is invention, liveness, and the unrepeatable human act.

The foil — the enemy is legacy

We define ourselves against the entire frozen, pre-planned old world of how things get made — never against any parent, partner, or peer. This is deliberate and load-bearing. The moment the brand defines itself against a sibling company, it rebuilds a bridge we have worked to burn.

Why now

When anything can be generated infinitely and instantly, the live and unrepeatable becomes the ultimate scarcity. You cannot prompt a session that already happened. And the deepest status on offer is no longer owning something rare — it is founding a medium.

The north star

Now — The Instrument. Sell the first instrument and the first sessions. Credible, invitation-only, tethered to what the platform can already do. Next — The Movement. Once the work proves itself, the studio becomes a school and the brand becomes a named movement. Origin, never nationalism — a birthplace story, not a flag.

03 — In Our DNA

The oldest new-medium instinct, aimed at a new frontier.

Improvisation isn't a metaphor we borrowed. It's the oldest new-medium instinct there is — and its greatest proof is jazz: the invention of Black American genius, the first time anyone built a complete art form live, without a score. Born in an American city and carried to the whole world. Rhapsody inherits that instinct and points it somewhere new — New Orleans to New York to orbit: made in one place, heard everywhere.

This is our root, not our costume. We honor it the only way that counts — by crediting it, and, where we can, by commissioning the artists who carry that lineage as founding voices of the first works.

The Placement Rule — how we hold the dose
DNA, not headline

Jazz lives in the origin story and the founder's voice — never in the name, the mark, the taglines, the rate card, or any iconography.

Always credited

When invoked, its Black American origin is named. We reference the principle of improvisation; we never appropriate the aesthetic of jazz.

Removable

The brand must stand fully with every jazz reference deleted. If removing it breaks the brand, it had become the emphasis.

The two states of the point

On paper — a solid point. Light grounds get a crisp, solid vermilion point. There is no halo, because a glow on a light surface reads as a smudge, not as light.

On ink — a luminous point. Dark grounds activate the halo: the point becomes a genuine light source emerging from the void. This is the only context where the glow is permitted.

05 — Construction & Misuse
Clear space & minimum size

Clear space = the cap-height of the "O" in PROJECT, on all four sides. Nothing enters this field — no type, no rule, no image edge.

Minimum size — Primary: 24 mm / 0.95 in print, 180 px digital. Emblem alone: 12 mm, 32 px. Below emblem minimums, use the app-icon crop.

The Signal rule

A precise mark of vermilion denotes focus, intent, and human presence. Use it to highlight, not to decorate. Maintain generous space around it. Never use it as a background, a large fill, or a container. Never replace it with another shape or color.

Pantone and CMYK values are nearest-match approximations for specification. Confirm against a physical press proof before production printing.

11 — Governance

The rules that keep the story intact.

Mass Silence Rule

Client-facing language speaks in sessions, works, windows, and moments — never in weight, capacity, or bus engineering. No $/kg anywhere, ever.

40% Capacity Cap

Long-term anchors are capped; the improvised, one-time spot sets carry the margin. The premium is the unrepeatable.

Data Plane Firewall

Every work runs on physically and cryptographically segregated networks. Client-facing translation: “your session is yours alone.”

GC Veto Gate

Curatorial selection is the screen. Every player passes GC review for zero overlap with active defense missions before the studio invites them to play.

Conversion Engine

Every PRELUDE-01 pilot receives the Adagio Conversion Credit — the “first session” that converts an experimenter into a resident.

The firewall

The identity reads as a wholly separate world from the parent's defense and aerospace business. No aerospace, satellite, defense, or “space-tech” visual language — ever.

Jazz stays DNA

Present, credited to its Black American origin, removable. Never the headline. Never iconography — no clefs, staves, notes, or soundwaves.

The name is scaffolding

“Project Rhapsody” is a codename pending trademark clearance in classes 35/38/41. The emblem is the durable asset and must survive a name change.

The test for any application: does it make an invited house feel like a founder of a new medium — and could it never be mistaken for the defense parent? If both aren't true, it isn't the one.
