## THE MARK

A fine horizon arc with a single point of light rising at its apex: the first light of a new medium. It carries frontier and first-ness.

**The point has two states:**
- **On light grounds**, a crisp solid vermilion point. No halo.
  A glow on a light surface reads as a smudge, not as light.
- **On dark grounds**, the halo activates and the point becomes a
  genuine light source. This is the only place the glow is permitted.

Use it to highlight, never to decorate. Never as a fill or background.
One signal moment per surface.

Pantone/CMYK are nearest-match approximations. Confirm on a press proof.

## USAGE ESSENTIALS

- **Clear space:** the cap-height of the "O" in PROJECT, all four sides.
- **Minimum size:** Primary: 24 mm / 180 px. Emblem: 12 mm / 32 px.
  Below emblem minimum, use the app-icon crop.
- **Never** stretch, recolor, re-typeset, rebuild the arc, add effects, outlines, shadows, or apply the glow on a light ground.
- The SVGs have **outlined lettering**. They render identically without
  the fonts installed. Always use the supplied files rather than
  re-typesetting the wordmark.
